# Code of Conduct

Barn Owl follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and
keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks
aren't accepted in issues, pull requests or anywhere else in the project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer,
[@marvika](https://github.com/marvika), privately. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments or block people who
don't follow this code.
